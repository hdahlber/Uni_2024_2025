## Variables and Measurements
The goal of this task is to understand how measurements are defined and used in empirical studies in CS&Eng. You need chapter 3 of the book by Wholin, two research articles that you will find in Moodle.

### Task 1.

Read the article by Per Runesson, Detection or Isolation of Defects? An Experimental Comparison of Unit Testing and Code Inspection. 

Identify  and report every variable they use in their study and define how it is measured. Include variable name, unit, scale type (section 3.1.1 in Wholin), if its objective or subjective (section 3.1.2) and if it is direct and indirect (section 3.1.3).

This task is rather easy since the authors of the article were kind enough to put most of the information in Table 1 of the article.
