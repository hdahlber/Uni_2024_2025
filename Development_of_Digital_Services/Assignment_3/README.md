### Group work 3: Service blueprint and business model + plans for testing

Create a service blueprint and a business model for your innovation:

You are free to choose the shape and elements for your service blueprint. However, your blueprint should map out the entire process of service delivery, above and below the line of visibility. You are also free to choose the tool you use to present your business model (e.g. the Business Model Canvas). The important thing is to ensure that your business model contains all the necessary elements to implement the process of service delivery as pictured in the service blueprint.

Create also a plan to test those elements of both your service blueprint and your business model. You may refer to your identified critical characteristics of your innovation as one of the testing tools. Most critically, you should identify the assumption you have made, rank the assumptions in order of their foreseen impact, and device a detailed plan to test whether the assumption holds.
