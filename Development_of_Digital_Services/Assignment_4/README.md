### Group work 4: User interface design

Create a wire frame for and a prototype of your innovation:

You are free to choose the tools you use for both the wire frame and the prototype. You are allowed to pick any tool you are comfortable with. You can even create the prototype as a paper mockup if you want. Here is a list of tools that you may find helpful in this task . We have tested the tools and we prefer these two (in order of preference):

1. Pencil (https://pencil.evolus.vn) FREE
2. Axure RP 9 (https://www.axure.com) Free 30 day trial

You may use any other tool if you please. At least the following tools are available for user interface design tasks: Moqups, Adobe XD, InVision, Origami, Sketch, Figma, and Webflow.

Please, submit your group work as a group in Moodle. Please, do report the names and student numbers of each participant to ensure that you will get the credit for your work. Do remember to include these details in all future group works as well.
