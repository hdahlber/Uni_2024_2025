## Group work 2: Critical characteristics of innovation

Choose one of the ideas you generated while playing the game "The Thing from the Future" (or choose some existing service) and apply the methods discussed on the fourth lecture (Critical characteristics of innovation) to find the set of modal salient beliefs that effectively determine the innovation's critical characteristics.

First, ask at least 20 people to list their salient beliefs about using the innovation and create a list of 5-9 modal salient beliefs. Report the list of modal salient beliefs and the steps you took from collecting the original lists of salient to forming the final list of modal salient beliefs. Consider carefully how you elicit the modal salient beliefs. You should clearly define the action (e.g. adopting, buying, using), target (your innovation) context (purpose of use) and time elements. 

Second, create a set of questions for assessing the relative impact of your identified modal salient beliefs (3-5 questions per belief) and a set of questions (at least three) for measuring the behavioral criterion (e.g. attitude toward adopting or intention to adopt your innovation). Ensure the correspondence in action, target, context, and time elements of all questions.
