Play multiple rounds of the game "The Thing from the Future" and discuss after each round not only the ideas you have generated but also to assumptions you made about the future when generating the ideas. Report in writing:

1)    the assumptions you have identified you have made when generating ideas, and

2)    a detailed description of the most innovative ideas you have come up with.

During this first round of group work, the focus is on generating novel ideas and identifying the assumptions that limit your ideation (thinking inside the box). In this round you should be generating 5G-related ideas as the fifth generation mobile networking is the technological context for the course this year.

Please, submit your group work as a group in Moodle. Because of this, please, do announce your group composition in the news forum of the group before trying to submit your work. To play it safe, also include in your report the names and student numbers of each participant to ensure that you will get the credit for your work. Do remember to include these details in all future group works as well.
